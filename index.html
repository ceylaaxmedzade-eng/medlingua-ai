Your blood sugar and cholesterol levels are slightly above normal. This doesn't mean you have diabetes or heart disease, but it's a sign to talk with your doctor about lifestyle adjustments. Ask about diet, exercise, and when to get tested again.

About MedLingua AI

MedLingua AI is a student-led project built at the intersection of medicine, public health, and artificial intelligence. Our mission: break down the language barrier between healthcare providers and patients.

We believe health literacy is a right, not a privilege. Whether you're reading your own report or helping a family member, clear language builds confidence and better conversations with doctors.

Research & Impact

Research agenda

I am a high school student from Azerbaijan aspiring to become a physician-scientist. My work sits at the intersection of medicine, public health and artificial intelligence, with a focus on how AI can improve health literacy for young people and families.

Growing up in a medical family, I have seen how often patients receive lab reports and diagnoses they cannot fully understand. This motivates me to design tools that reduce fear, support informed decisions and make high-quality medical knowledge accessible regardless of background, language or income.

Core interests

  • AI-driven simplification of medical language
  • Health literacy in adolescents and young adults
  • Public health communication and misinformation
  • Equity in access to understandable medical information
  • Human-centered AI for clinical and educational settings

Current projects

MedLingua AI: Plain-language medical explanations

MedLingua AI is a web-based prototype that transforms complex medical terminology into clear, emotionally safe language for patients and their families. The project explores:

  • NLP approaches to medical text simplification
  • How tone (neutral, gentle, direct) affects anxiety
  • Reducing “Google panic” and self-diagnosis
  • Supporting better conversations between doctors and patients

The long-term goal is to collaborate with clinicians, public health organisations and youth health initiatives to pilot MedLingua AI in real-world settings.

Study: Health literacy in adolescents

This independent study focuses on how teenagers interpret common medical tests and health information. It examines:

  • Main sources of health information (social media, family, doctors)
  • Levels of medical anxiety and common misconceptions
  • Whether AI-assisted explanations can improve understanding and reduce fear

The study is planned as a mixed-methods project combining questionnaires, scenario-based tasks and qualitative feedback from participants.

AI, environment and public health (concept stage)

Building on my interest in public health and environmental exposures, I am developing concepts for projects that connect air quality, urban living and cardiovascular and mental health in young people.

The idea is to use data-driven tools and AI to visualise risks in a way that teenagers and families can easily understand and act on, especially in rapidly developing cities.

Why this matters

Around the world, millions of people receive medical reports, discharge summaries and treatment plans they cannot fully understand. Misunderstanding leads to unnecessary panic, delayed care, misuse of medication and widening health inequalities.

Clear, respectful communication is not a luxury; it is a public health priority. By combining AI with evidence-based communication strategies, I aim to create tools that support patients rather than overwhelm them.
